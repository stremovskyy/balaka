Stress and pronunciation

        

          Ukrainian: OmniVoice 0.2.1 does not document a reliable way to mark word stress. Accent marks, capitals, and + may be ignored; test important words in a short sample.
        

        
English: replace a word with CMU phonemes in brackets. Digits mark syllable stress.

        Play the [B EY1 S] guitar.
        
Chinese: use uppercase pinyin with a tone number where needed.

        这批货打ZHE2出售。
      


      

        
Pauses and phrasing

        
Use commas, periods, question marks, and ellipses to suggest natural phrasing.

        Hello, friends. Today... we begin.
        

          There is no documented [pause], <break>, or SSML command for an exact pause inside speech. Edge silence is controlled separately by “Edge silence” in generation settings.
        

      


      

        
Expressive sounds

        
Insert supported tags directly into the text. Start with [laughter] or [sigh].

        That was funny [laughter].
        
All documented tags:

        [laughter] [sigh] [confirmation-en] [question-en] [question-ah] [question-oh] [question-ei] [question-yi] [surprise-ah] [surprise-oh] [surprise-wa] [surprise-yo] [dissatisfaction-hnn]
        
The model may skip or vary these sounds, especially outside its strongest training languages.

      


      

        
Numbers and voice style

        
For important numbers, write the spoken words yourself; automatic number normalization is not enabled in Balaka.

        сто двадцять три instead of 123
        
Use the voice and generation settings for gender, age, pitch, whisper, speed, and duration. Voice cloning follows the reference audio and its transcript.
